# AI Career Roadmap Generator

## 🚀 Overview

AI Career Roadmap Generator is an AI-powered career guidance platform designed to help students, freshers, and professionals plan their career growth effectively. The system analyzes a user's skills, education, interests, and target job role to generate a personalized learning roadmap, identify skill gaps, recommend certifications, and suggest industry-relevant projects.

By leveraging Generative AI, the platform provides structured career guidance and helps users make informed decisions about their learning journey and professional development.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many students and fresh graduates struggle to identify the skills, certifications, and projects required to achieve their desired career goals. Due to the rapidly changing technology landscape, learners often follow unstructured learning paths, resulting in skill gaps and reduced employability.

The AI Career Roadmap Generator addresses this challenge by creating personalized career roadmaps tailored to individual goals and industry requirements.

---

## 💡 Solution Overview

The platform uses Generative AI to:

* Analyze user profiles and resumes
* Identify skill gaps
* Recommend certifications
* Suggest hands-on projects
* Generate personalized learning roadmaps
* Track career progress

Users receive a structured plan that guides them from their current skill level to their desired career role.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### Resume Analysis

Extracts skills, education, and experience from uploaded resumes.

### Skill Gap Analysis

Identifies missing skills required for the target job role.

### Personalized Career Roadmap

Creates a step-by-step learning and career development plan.

### Certification Recommendations

Suggests relevant certifications based on career objectives.

### Project Recommendations

Provides industry-relevant project ideas to strengthen portfolios.

### Learning Resource Suggestions

Provides personalized career guidance and recommendations.

## 📌 Workflow

1. User enters profile details or uploads resume.
2. System extracts skills and educational information.
3. AI performs skill-gap analysis.
4. Gemini generates a personalized roadmap.
5. Certifications and projects are recommended.
6. Roadmap is displayed to the user.
7. Progress can be tracked and updated over time.
